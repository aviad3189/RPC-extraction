Aviad Eisenberg, Sharon Gannot, and Shlomo E. Chazan
Faculty of Engineering, Bar-Ilan University, Ramat-Gan, Israel · OriginAI, Israel

The target speaker is selected by where they are relative to the other speakers, for example “the speaker to the left of the interferer” or “the closer speaker”, without a direction-of-arrival estimate or an enrollment recording. Each mixture below is processed once with the command of each speaker. The same network, given a different command, returns a different speaker.

About these examples

  • All mixtures are from the test sets of the paper: speakers, noise signals, and rooms were not seen during training.
  • Examples were chosen automatically. Among the test mixtures in which the speech fills the whole 4 s and the SNR is at most 12 dB, we show the one with the highest mean output SI-SDR over all outputs on this page (both models, both array sizes, and both commands). They are therefore better than average; the average results are reported in the paper.
  • Signals are sampled at 8 kHz and are 4 s long. The models extract the reverberant target at the reference microphone; the references are the reverberant images of each speaker.
  • Every clip is loudness-normalized for listening. SI-SDR values are computed against the reference of the commanded speaker; “other speaker” is the SI-SDR of the same output against the other speaker.
  • “Left” and “right” are defined from the array's point of view: a larger direction of arrival is further to the left.
